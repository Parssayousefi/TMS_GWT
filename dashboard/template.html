Corrected analyses from Aachen (15 participants) and SUDMEX (40 participants). A shared PID treatment effect has not yet been established. The current investigation concerns information processing and workspace effects; haemodynamic associations and repeatability analyses remain separate records and are not the publication narrative.

Six processing modes: no shared treatment effect established

The follow-up separates storage, transfer, copying, erasure and the two directions of joint–individual conversion. All six modes were tested in seven fixed pair groups, in raw information units and with separately calibrated conditional normalization.

The smallest unadjusted SUDMEX baseline-and-motion-adjusted p-value is .347 for raw modes and .350 for normalized modes. None of the cross-dataset conjunctions survives Holm6 or Holm42. Regional change templates learned in one cohort also failed to predict the other cohort’s response. These tests do not establish equivalence or preservation.

Why the normalized DAN interval excludes zero

In Aachen, the mean normalized DAN gateway count falls from 1.43 to 0.97. The unadjusted t-test gives p = .0382; Holm4 gives p = .1527. The exact sign-flip test gives p = .0566 before correction and maxT4 p = .1621. Nine participants decrease, five are unchanged and one increases.

The independent audit reconstructed all counts from the saved scan-level tables and reproduced the figure and all four-outcome tests. SUDMEX's normalized adjusted contrast is +0.400 gateways (p = .422), so this does not establish a shared reduction. Nonsignificant changes are not evidence of unchanged or preserved architecture.

Read the estimand before the p-value

Aachen measures within-person change without sham. SUDMEX acute tests compare randomized arms. Clinical and HRF associations, method interactions, all-post summaries and leave-one-out diagnostics answer different questions. Raw information measures use nats; participation coefficients are dimensionless and gateway metrics are counts. Normalized information values are conditional-reference quantiles; they are not in nats. Directed target measures were not normalized using symmetric-measure references.

Sampling stability

Each condition has 2,000 participant bootstraps and 1,000 split halves. Aachen also includes every participant omission. Aachen between-baseline Dice is .782 (gradient ρ = .716); matched SUDMEX sham between-visit Dice is .803 (ρ = .790). No overlap threshold or participant exclusion was selected from these results.

What this cannot certify

Regional functional coverage remains uneven, especially in inferior frontal and temporal parcels. Workspace similarity does not certify temporal signal quality at every parcel. Deposited Aachen seeds are analysis ROIs; exact coil or peak-field location is not independently verified.
